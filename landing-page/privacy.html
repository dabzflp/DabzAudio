Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Cookie settings

DabzAudio ("we", "us") provides browser-based tools for musicians, producers and DJs. This policy explains what personal data we handle and how we use cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage), following best-practice privacy standards including the UK GDPR and PECR.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — the email, password (stored only as a secure hash), artist name, unique @username, country and optional profile picture you provide for the Lyric Book.
  • Your content — lyrics you create, collaborators you invite, and Playback releases and tracks (audio and cover art) you upload.
  • Files you transfer — files sent with “Transfer a file”, along with the sender name, message and recipient emails you enter. Transfers and their files are automatically deleted 7 days after they are created.
  • Drop Pilot (Pro) — the release details, campaign plans, pitch drafts and press kit you create, plus any song audio or artwork you upload. Drop Pilot does not analyse your audio and does not distribute your music. We also record how many AI credits you use (counts only, not your text).
  • Split sheets & invoices — the details you enter, including the names and emails of the people you send them to.
  • Gift, invoice & subscription payments — payments are processed by Stripe or Paystack. We never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Feedback & newsletter — the name, email and message you submit through the forms on the home page.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

Essential. These are required for the site to function and don't need consent.

NameTypePurpose
lb_token Cookie / local storage Keeps you signed in and secures your Lyric Book session (up to 30 days or until you sign out).
dabz-theme Local storage Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
dabz_cookie_consent Local storage Remembers your Accept all / Reject non-essential choice for advertising cookies.
lb_pending_invite Local storage Holds a collaboration invite while you sign in or create an account, then is removed.
lb_badge, dz_agent_seen Session storage Caches your initials badge and remembers you've seen the Ask Dabz assistant. Cleared when you close the tab.

Advertising (optional, only with your consent). The home page and the Key & BPM and Reverb & Delay tool pages can show Google AdSense ads. Google AdSense is not loaded unless you click Accept all on our cookie banner. If you accept, Google may set its own cookies and use identifiers to serve and measure ads. We do not control these cookies; see how Google uses data from sites that use its services. If you click Reject non-essential, no advertising script or cookies are loaded. You can change your choice at any time: Cookie settings. The Lyric Book itself does not show ads or use advertising cookies.

Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to download them.

To clear what we store, sign out or clear this site's data in your browser settings. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser.

3. How we use your data

To run your account, store and share your lyrics, files, split sheets and invoices, process payments, send the emails you trigger (invites, transfer links, receipts and password resets), and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data.

AI features. When you use the AI assistant or Smart search, the lyric text and prompt you submit are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to produce a response. We use this only to answer your request.

Drop Pilot. When you generate a campaign or draft a pitch, the release details and any lyrics you entered are sent to our AI provider to write the result. We keep only the token count and credits spent for billing and abuse limits, not the prompt or the reply.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that make DabzAudio work, and only as needed to deliver the service or where required by law:

  • Hosting and database (Railway)
  • Media and file storage (Cloudinary)
  • Email delivery (Resend)
  • Payments (Stripe, Paystack)
  • AI processing (Groq); optional speech synthesis (Google Cloud Text-to-Speech)
  • Forms (Formspree)
  • Advertising on public pages (Google AdSense)

Links you create (for example a transfer link or a Playback share link) can be opened by anyone who has them, so only share them with people you trust.

5. Retention

Transfers are deleted after 7 days. Your account data and content are kept until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account removes your lyrics and related data, including your Drop Pilot campaigns.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time. To make a request, contact us below.
