Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Cookie Policy · Cookie settings

DabzAudio (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides browser-based tools and services for musicians, producers, songwriters, and DJs, including Key & BPM Analysis, Reverb & Delay Calculator, Lyric Book, Drop Pilot, and the DabzAudio Community Hub.

For data protection purposes under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, DabzAudio (United Kingdom) is the data controller for personal data processed through our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile details — information you provide when registering or managing an account, such as your email address, cryptographically hashed password, display or artist name, username, country or region, optional profile image, and multi-factor authentication settings if enabled.
  • Your creative content and workspaces — lyrics, project notes, collaborator invitations, Playback releases and audio/artwork uploads, and Drop Pilot release plans, pitches, and press kit materials that you choose to create or upload.
  • File transfers — files you upload to send via Transfer files, together with the sender name, optional message, and recipient email addresses you enter. Transfers and their associated files are automatically deleted 7 days after creation.
  • Split sheets, invoices, and Gift Me — transaction and agreement details you enter, including recipient names, email addresses, descriptions, and payment status records.
  • Community Hub contributions — forum posts, comments, categories, and the display name (or anonymous attribution) you choose to publish in public community areas.
  • Payments and subscriptions — paid plans, invoices, and Gift Me transactions are handled by third-party payment processors (Stripe and Paystack). We receive payment status and billing metadata needed to manage your access, and never collect or store your full card or bank account numbers.
  • Browser audio tools — audio files or microphone input used in the Key & BPM tool or calculators are processed solely to provide your immediate analysis result and are not added to an account library.
  • Support, feedback, and newsletter — your name, email address, subject, and message when you contact us or subscribe via our forms.
  • Technical and security logs — standard connection metadata (such as IP address, browser user agent, request timestamps, and diagnostic errors) needed to keep the service reliable, secure, and free from abuse.

2. Lawful bases for processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases depending on the context:

  • Contract: to register your account and provide the tools, storage, collaboration, transfers, and paid features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure our platform, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain reliability, and moderate public community areas, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: where you opt in to non-essential advertising cookies on public pages or subscribe to optional updates (you can withdraw consent at any time).
  • Legal obligation: to meet applicable financial, tax, accounting, or statutory compliance duties.

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data to operate and maintain your account, deliver requested tools and collaboration features, send transactional emails you trigger (such as verification, invitations, transfer notices, receipts, and password resets), process payments, and protect platform integrity. We do not sell your personal data.

AI-assisted features: When you choose to use AI features (such as Smart search, lyric suggestions, or Drop Pilot campaign/pitch drafting), the prompt or content you submit is sent to an AI processing provider solely to generate your requested output.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with trusted service providers acting on our instructions, or where required by law. Depending on which features you use, recipients fall into the following categories:

  • Cloud hosting, compute, and database infrastructure providers
  • Media and file storage providers
  • Transactional email delivery providers
  • Payment processors (Stripe and Paystack)
  • AI processing and optional speech synthesis providers (only when you invoke those features)
  • Form submission providers for public feedback and newsletter forms
  • Advertising partners (Google AdSense) on public pages, only when you give cookie consent

Public & shared content: Links you create and share (such as file transfer links or Playback share links) can be viewed by anyone who has the link, so only share them with people you trust. Content posted to the Community Hub is publicly visible.

5. International data transfers

Some of our service providers operate outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we use legally recognized safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or approved standard contractual protections.

6. Data retention

  • File transfers: automatically deleted 7 days after creation.
  • Account data and workspace content: kept while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Deleting your account removes your profile and stored creative content, subject to limited records we must retain for legal, tax, or payment compliance.
  • Technical and security logs: retained only for a limited operational period needed for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, access controls, and optional multi-factor authentication. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and keep shared links private.

8. Minimum age and children’s privacy

  • General tools: Our public browser tools can be used without creating an account.
  • Accounts & community: Account-based and community features are intended for individuals aged 13 or older (or the minimum age required for digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA, unless a parent or legal guardian provides consent where permitted by law).
  • Paid & financial features: Paid subscriptions, Gift Me, invoices, and split-sheet agreements require you to be 18 or older (or acting with valid parent or legal guardian authorization).

If you believe a younger child has provided personal data without required consent, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

For full details on essential storage, optional advertising cookies, and how to change your preferences at any time, please read our Cookie Policy or open Cookie settings.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to, correction of, export (portability) of, restriction of, or deletion of your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or your local data protection authority.

11. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or account/data inquiries: dabzaudio@dabzflp.com.
