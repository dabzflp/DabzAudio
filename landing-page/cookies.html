Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Privacy Policy · Cookie settings

This Cookie Policy explains how DabzAudio (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies (such as local storage and session storage) across our websites and tools, in line with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

You can review or change your advertising cookie choice at any time:

1. Strictly necessary (essential) technologies

These cookies and browser storage items are required for the core operation, security, and preferences you explicitly request. Because they are strictly necessary, they remain active without requiring advertising consent.

Category Type Purpose Duration
Authentication & session security Cookie / browser storage Keeps you signed in to protected areas and helps verify and protect your active session. Up to 30 days or until you sign out
Cookie consent preference Browser storage Records your Accept all / Reject non-essential selection so we respect your choice across visits. Persistent until cleared or updated
Appearance preference Browser storage Remembers your light or dark theme preference across DabzAudio pages. Persistent until cleared
Workflow & interface state Browser / session storage Temporarily holds pending collaboration invites or lightweight interface state while you sign in or navigate. Temporary (cleared after use or when you close the tab)

2. Optional advertising cookies (consent required)

Our public landing page and free browser tool pages (such as Key & BPM Analysis and the Reverb & Delay Calculator) may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

  • No ads before consent: The Google AdSense script is not loaded unless you click Accept all in the cookie banner.
  • If you accept: Google may set cookies and use device identifiers to serve, personalize, and measure ads. We do not control third-party cookies set by Google; learn more at how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • If you reject or withdraw consent: Choosing Reject non-essential prevents advertising scripts from loading, and switching from Accept to Reject clears reachable advertising cookies and reloads the page without ads.
  • Workspace apps: Authenticated Lyric Book pages do not load advertising scripts or advertising cookies.

3. Web fonts

Some pages load typefaces from external font providers (such as Google Fonts). When your browser downloads those font files, it makes a standard request to the font provider’s servers; no advertising cookies are placed by doing so.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

In addition to our Cookie settings control, you can clear stored site data or block third-party cookies through your browser’s privacy settings. Please note that clearing or blocking strictly necessary storage may sign you out or reset your theme and consent preferences.

5. More information & contact

To learn more about how we handle personal data and your rights, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us at dabzaudio@dabzflp.com.
